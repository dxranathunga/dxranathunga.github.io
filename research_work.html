Drug design and delivery

Implant osseointegration in diabetic conditions

Used molecular dynamics to investigate a drug-delivery approach intended to induce implant osseointegration in diabetic conditions, with an emphasis on HMGB1 delivery and titanium-surface interactions.

Molecular dynamicsEnhanced samplingProtein bindingTitanium surfacesIonic-liquid coatings
  • Designed structural isoforms of HMGB1 and studied pre-delivery and post-implantation release scenarios.
  • Evaluated free-energy profiles of protein folding and protein-binding free energies with enhanced-sampling methods.
  • Studied the influence of ions and water on drug adsorption induced by titanium-implant surface modifications.
  • Characterized dicationic imidazolium-based ionic-liquid coatings on titanium surfaces for HMGB1 drug delivery.
