About

From physical models to practical research platforms.

I develop computational methods that help scientists understand molecular interactions, prioritize compounds, and make better decisions across drug discovery, drug delivery, formulation, biophysics, materials, and engineering. My work combines physical chemistry, molecular simulation, data-driven modeling, and software engineering.
